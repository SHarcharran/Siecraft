What to Engrave on a Tumbler: 50 Ideas for Every Occasion

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

You've picked the tumbler. Now what do you put on it? Here are 50 ideas organized by occasion.

For Weddings & Bridesmaids

  • The bride's name + wedding date
  • Each bridesmaid's name + role (Maid of Honor, Bridesmaid, Flower Girl)
  • A quote she loves
  • The wedding hashtag
  • Coordinates of where you met

Bridesmaid tumblers are our #1 wedding order. They work because every bridesmaid uses a tumbler every day. Every sip is a reminder.

For Birthdays

  • Their name + birth year ("Sarah · 1990")
  • A quote they live by
  • Their astrological sign
  • A funny inside joke
  • Their hobby or passion (e.g. "Dog Mom" or "Gym Rat")

Birthday tumblers are our most re-ordered product. Customers come back every year for a new one for a different friend.

For Corporate Gifts

  • Company logo
  • Employee name + title
  • Company name + "Team [Year]"
  • A motivational phrase
  • Department or team name

Logo tumblers make excellent corporate gifts because they're useful, visible, and keep your brand in front of the recipient every day.

For Holidays

  • Family name + "Christmas [Year]"
  • "Thankful" or "Grateful" with a name
  • "Hot Cocoa Season" with snowflakes
  • Holiday quote + name
  • New Year: "Hello [Year]" + their name
