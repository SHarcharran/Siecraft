The 7 Best Housewarming Gifts for Orlando Home Buyers

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

Orlando has one of the hottest real estate markets in the country. Here's what's actually working as housewarming gifts for new Florida homeowners.

Why Generic Gifts Don't Work Anymore

The average new homeowner in Orlando gets a candle, a bottle of wine, and a gift card to Home Depot. None of these things are memorable. What people remember is something personalized — something that was made specifically for their new home.

The 7 Best Gifts

  • Cedar Address Plaque ($45–$90) — Goes on the front porch. Shows the house number and street name. Every neighbor and visitor sees it.
  • Family Name Sign ($55–$110) — "The Johnson Family" in the entryway. Classic.
  • Established Sign ($55–$95) — "Est. 2025" with their last name. Goes above the fireplace or in the living room.
  • Personalized Cutting Board ($45–$75) — Something they'll use every day in their new kitchen.
  • Engraved Tumbler with New Address ($38–$50) — Unexpected and creative.
  • Set of Personalized Coasters ($30–$50) — For the new living room or home bar.
  • Custom House Portrait Sign ($75–$130) — CNC-carved outline of their house style with their name and address.

For Realtors: What Clients Talk About Most

We work with realtors across Orlando. The unanimous feedback: clients talk about the address plaque for months after closing. It's the one gift that stays in the home, visible every day. One realtor told us a client texted her a photo of it installed the same afternoon they picked up the keys.
