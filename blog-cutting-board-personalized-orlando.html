Why Personalized Cutting Boards Are the Ultimate Kitchen Gift

A cutting board is something used almost daily. When it's personalized with someone's name, a wedding date, or a family name — it becomes an heirloom. We've made cutting boards for weddings in 2019 that are still the most-used item in their kitchen. This is a gift that lasts decades.

What We Engrave on Cutting Boards

  • Family name + "Est. [Year]"
  • Couple's names + wedding date
  • Recipe in their handwriting (photo-engraved)
  • State outline with a heart where they met
  • Custom logo for a restaurant or food business
  • New baby's name + birth details
  • Anniversary milestone ("10 Years Married")

Bamboo vs Hardwood Cutting Boards

We use both bamboo and maple cutting boards. Bamboo is harder than most wood (Janka rating equivalent to oak), very smooth, and takes laser engraving beautifully. Maple is the traditional cutting board wood — great grain, durable, food-safe. Both options are food-safe and available.
