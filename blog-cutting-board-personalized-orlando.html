Why Personalized Cutting Boards Are the Ultimate Kitchen Gift

A cutting board is something used almost daily. When it's personalized with someone's name, a wedding date, or a family name — it becomes an heirloom. This is a gift that lasts decades.

What We Engrave on Cutting Boards

  • Family name + "Est. [Year]"
  • Couple's names + wedding date
  • Recipe in their handwriting (send us a clear scan)
  • State outline with a heart where they met
  • Custom logo for a restaurant or food business
  • New baby's name + birth details
  • Anniversary milestone ("10 Years Married")

Why We Use Maple

Our cutting and serving boards are solid hard maple, the traditional cutting board wood. It has a tight grain, holds up to daily use, is food-safe, and takes engraving cleanly.
