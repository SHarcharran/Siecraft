Wood vs Acrylic Ornaments

Wood ornaments (typically birch or maple) have a warm, natural look and are extremely light. Acrylic ornaments can be transparent or opaque and catch light differently — they can look almost like crystal when well-designed. We offer both; your choice based on the aesthetic you prefer.
