Memorial Sign Wording Ideas: What to Put on a Memorial Plaque

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

What you write on a memorial sign matters as much as the wood and the craftsmanship. Here are wording ideas organized by type of loss and desired tone.

Classic Memorial Wording

  • In loving memory of [Name] · [Birth Year]–[Death Year]
  • Forever in our hearts · [Name]
  • [Name] · A life well lived · [Year]–[Year]
  • Gone but never forgotten · [Name]
  • With us always · [Name]

For a Garden Memorial

  • [Name]'s Garden · A place of peace
  • In memory of [Name] · This garden grows in your honor
  • [Pet's name] ran here · Forever free
  • A corner of [Name]'s world · Tended in memory

Personal & Meaningful

  • His words: "[quote he always said]"
  • She taught us that "[a lesson she lived by]"
  • Built this house, built this family · [Name] · [Years]
  • Still fishing from the best spot · [Name]

For Pet Memorials

  • [Pet's name] · A good dog / cat · [Year]–[Year]
  • Best boy / girl forever · [Name]
  • [Name] · You filled this house with joy
  • Always by our side, forever in our hearts · [Name]

How to Send Wording to SieCraft

When you order a memorial sign, include your chosen wording in the order notes. We'll lay it out in our design software and send you a proof before cutting. If you're unsure what to write, tell us about the person or pet and we'll suggest options. We do this work with care.
