Cedar vs Pine vs Walnut: Which Wood for Your Custom Sign?

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

Choosing the wrong wood is the most common custom sign mistake. Here's the complete breakdown.

Cedar — The Outdoor King

  • Naturally weather and insect resistant
  • Handles Florida heat, rain, and humidity
  • Develops a beautiful silver-gray patina unsealed
  • Annual sealant extends life to 20+ years
  • Best for: address plaques, porch signs, garden signs, outdoor business signs

Cedar is our default recommendation for anything going outdoors. Florida's climate is brutal on wood, and cedar is built for it. A cedar address plaque we sealed and installed in 2022 looks exactly the same today.

Pine — The Painted Sign Champion

  • Lightest color — pale yellow-white when natural
  • Most affordable option
  • Best for painted or fully-stained signs
  • Doesn't have dramatic grain (less interesting natural)
  • Best for: signs that will be fully painted, kids' room signs, quote signs with heavy paint

If your sign is going to be painted a solid color, pine is the smart choice. The grain won't show through the paint, and you save money on material.

Walnut — The Indoor Showpiece

  • Deep chocolate brown with dramatic lighter streaks
  • The grain itself is the design element
  • High-contrast engraving (lighter wood shows through dark)
  • Not recommended for direct outdoor exposure
  • Best for: indoor family signs, office awards, memorial plaques, living room wall art

Walnut is our most commented-on material. Clients who haven't seen walnut engraving before are always surprised by how rich it looks. The engraving stands out beautifully.
