Why Buying Local From an Orlando Craftsman Beats Etsy and Amazon

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

You can order a "custom" wood sign on Amazon and have it in two days. Here's why that's not actually what you think it is — and why buying local from SieCraft is different.

What Amazon "Custom" Signs Actually Are

Most "personalized" items on Amazon are produced in large fulfillment centers using automated machines with minimal human oversight. Your name is printed or lightly engraved by software with no human reviewing the design. Returns for quality issues are common. The price is low because the quality is low.

What Etsy "Handmade" Actually Means

Etsy allows products made in factories to be sold as handmade as long as the shop owner had some creative input in the design. Many Etsy sellers are resellers of overseas-manufactured products with custom printing applied. Not all — but buyer beware.

What SieCraft Actually Does

Every order that comes through SieCraft is reviewed by a real person. The design is done on our machine locally. The wood was selected and prepared by hand. We sand, finish, and quality-check each piece before it leaves the shop. You can come pick it up, meet us at a market, and see the workshop. That's not possible with Amazon.

The Real Difference: Quality and Communication

When you order from SieCraft, you can text (929) 371-0501 and get a real person who knows your order. If something isn't right in the proof, we fix it before cutting. If the sign has a defect, we remake it. That level of accountability simply doesn't exist in mass-market ordering.
