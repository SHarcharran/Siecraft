Pet Memorial Signs: Honoring Your Pet With Something Lasting

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

Losing a pet is one of the hardest experiences. A personalized pet memorial sign gives you something lasting to honor them — and a place to put your grief.

What We Make for Pet Memorials

  • Walnut indoor memorial plaque with their name, breed, and years
  • Cedar outdoor garden sign to mark a special spot in the yard
  • Laser-engraved pet portrait on wood from your favorite photo
  • Custom paw print sign with name and dates
  • Garden stone marker (engraved acrylic stake)

The most meaningful pieces we make are often the pet ones. Because the bond with a pet is completely unconditional — and people want to honor that.

Photo Engraving for Pet Portraits

Send us a clear photo of your pet — preferably a portrait with good contrast, looking toward the camera. We convert it to a high-contrast engraving format and laser it into a walnut or maple plank. The result is a portrait that will outlast any printed photo.

For Pet Owners Who Want to Honor Their Pet's Space

Many customers ask for a garden sign to mark where their pet loved to nap, play, or was laid to rest. A cedar sign reading "[Pet's Name] · 2012–2024 · Forever in our hearts" in a garden is a quiet, beautiful tribute that stays long after memory fades.
