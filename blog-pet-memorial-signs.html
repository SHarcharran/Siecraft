Pet Memorial Signs: Honoring Your Pet With Something Lasting

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

Losing a pet is one of the hardest experiences. A personalized pet memorial sign gives you something lasting to honor them — and a place to put your grief.

What We Make for Pet Memorials

  • Walnut indoor memorial plaque with their name, breed, and years
  • Cedar outdoor garden sign to mark a special spot in the yard
  • CNC V-carved photo plaque from your favorite photo of them
  • Custom paw print sign with name and dates
  • Garden marker (carved wood stake, sealed for outdoors)

The bond with a pet is unconditional, and a memorial sign is a way to honor it.

V-Carved Photo Plaques

Send us a clear photo of your pet — preferably a close portrait with good contrast, looking toward the camera. We convert it into a carving file and V-carve it into the wood on our CNC. You see a proof before we cut.

For Pet Owners Who Want to Honor Their Pet's Space

A garden sign can mark where a pet loved to nap, play, or was laid to rest. A cedar sign reading "[Pet's Name] · 2012–2024 · Forever in our hearts" in a garden is a quiet, beautiful tribute that stays long after memory fades.
