Best Craft Markets in Orlando to Find Handmade Goods (& Find SieCraft)

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

Orlando has a growing craft market scene with genuine handmade vendors. Here's where to find us and the other talented makers in Central Florida.

Lake Eola Farmer's Market — Our Favorite

The Sunday Farmer's Market at Lake Eola Park is one of Orlando's best. It runs every Sunday from 10am to 4pm rain or shine (mostly). There's a solid mix of produce, food trucks, and handmade vendors. We've had a booth here and the community vibe is excellent.

Winter Park Farmers' Market

Saturday mornings in Winter Park's Central Park area. A more upscale market with a loyal local following. Great for one-of-a-kind handmade pieces and specialty food vendors.

Orlando Flea Market

Off Highway 192, the Orlando Flea Market runs Friday through Sunday and features hundreds of vendors. More variety than a traditional craft market, good for finding handmade items at all price points.
