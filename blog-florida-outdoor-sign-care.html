How to Care for an Outdoor Wood Sign in Florida (Heat, Rain & Humidity)

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

Florida is one of the hardest climates for outdoor wood signs. High UV, daily afternoon rain, and constant humidity accelerate wear. Here's how to make yours last decades.

Why Florida is Different

Florida's combination of intense UV radiation, daily summer rain, and year-round humidity is harder on outdoor wood than almost any other US climate. A sign that would last 10 years in Minnesota might last 3 years in Florida without proper care.

Year 1 Maintenance

Your sign shipped with two coats of outdoor sealant. You need nothing for the first 12 months. Enjoy it.

Annual Re-Sealing (Most Important Step)

  • Buy Thompson's WaterSeal or similar water-based outdoor sealant
  • Wipe the sign clean with a dry cloth and let it dry completely (don't seal a wet sign)
  • Apply one thin coat with a foam brush in even strokes
  • Let dry for 24 hours before re-hanging outdoors
  • Do this every 12 months — more often for signs in direct sun or rain

This single step is the difference between a sign that looks great at 15 years and one that's cracking at 5.

What to Avoid

  • Pressure washing — strips sealant and can damage engraving
  • Leaving it flat where water pools
  • Direct sprinkler contact
  • Painting over the engraving
  • Leaving it unsealed through a Florida summer
