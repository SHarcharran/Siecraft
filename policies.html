Orders, Refunds, Shipping & Privacy

SieCraft LLC📍 Orlando, FLUpdated November 2026

Plain-English policies for every SieCraft order. Questions? Text or call (929) 371-0501, or email SieCraftcnc@gmail.com.

How an Order Works

  • You tell us what you want through the quote form or by placing a deposit online.
  • We send you a free digital design proof and confirm the exact price and timeline.
  • Nothing is cut until you approve the proof. You can request changes before you approve.
  • Starting prices on the site are minimums. Your exact price is confirmed with your proof.

Deposits & Refunds

  • Custom orders paid online start with a 50% deposit on the starting price. It goes toward your final total.
  • Your deposit is fully refundable until you approve your design proof. If you don't like the proof and decide not to go ahead, tell us and we'll refund the full deposit to your original payment method.
  • Once you approve the proof, we start making your piece, and the deposit is applied to your order.
  • The remaining balance is due when your piece is finished, before pickup or shipping.
  • If there's a problem with a finished piece, contact us with a photo and we'll work out a fair solution with you.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.

Buy Now Items

  • Buy Now items (like ornaments, keychains, coasters and garden stakes) are personalized and made after you order. You pay the full price at checkout.
  • We make your piece with the exact text you enter at checkout. If something looks unclear, we'll contact you before we cut.
  • Changed your mind? Contact us before we start making it for a full refund.
  • Because each piece is personalized, it can't be returned. If we made a mistake or it arrives damaged, contact us with a photo and we'll make it right.

Pickup & Shipping

  • Local pickup in Orlando is free. We'll arrange a pickup time with you.
  • We ship anywhere in the US by USPS or UPS. Buy Now items ship for a flat $6; shipping for custom orders is quoted with your order.
  • We confirm your timeline with your proof. Have a deadline? Tell us the date when you request a quote.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order:

  • What we collect: your name, email, phone number, order details, any photos or logos you send, and your shipping address if we ship to you.
  • How we use it: to quote, design, make, deliver and support your order, and to contact you about it. We don't send marketing emails unless you ask for them.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Services we use: Stripe (payments), Resend (order emails) and Vercel (website hosting). They process data only to provide those services.
  • We never sell your information.
  • Your photos: used only for your order. We'll ask before showing your piece in our gallery.
  • Your choices: email SieCraftcnc@gmail.com to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
