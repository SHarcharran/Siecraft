Types of Memorial Pieces We Make

Every memorial piece we make is personal. We take time with each one — the design, the wood selection, the finish. Here is what we offer:

  • Indoor memorial plaques in walnut or cedar
  • Outdoor garden signs (weather-sealed cedar)
  • CNC V-carved photo plaques from your photos
  • Pet memorial signs and plaques
  • Memorial bench plates (we supply the plate)
  • Garden memorial stakes (carved wood, sealed for outdoors)

We understand that sometimes timing is urgent. If you need a memorial piece for a service, a burial, or a gathering, call or text us at (929) 371-0501 with the date. We'll tell you honestly whether we can make it before you order.

V-Carved Photo Plaques

We can turn a photo into a V-carved wood plaque on our CNC. Send us a clear, high-contrast photo and we'll prepare it for carving and send you a proof. The result is a detailed portrait carved directly into the wood — no ink, no paper. Best on maple or walnut.

Indoor vs Outdoor Memorial Signs

For indoor display — living rooms, offices, hallways — we recommend walnut. The dark grain is stunning and the piece will look beautiful for decades with minimal care. For outdoor garden memorials, we recommend cedar, sealed for outdoor use. Cedar handles Florida weather naturally without rotting.
