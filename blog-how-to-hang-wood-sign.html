How to Hang a Wood Sign Without Damaging Your Wall

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

Most wood signs come with mounting hardware — here's exactly how to hang yours cleanly, level, and without damaging your drywall.

What's Included with Your SieCraft Sign

  • Keyhole routing on the back (for screws)
  • D-ring hooks with screws
  • Sawtooth hanger for lighter pieces
  • Twine or leather cord for tags

Every SieCraft sign ships with mounting hardware appropriate for its size and weight. We'll note what's included in your order confirmation.

Step-by-Step: Hanging on Drywall

  • Use a stud finder to locate studs — anchor into a stud whenever possible for heavy signs
  • If no stud is available, use a drywall anchor rated for your sign's weight
  • Use a level to mark your screw position before drilling
  • For two-screw signs, measure the exact distance between keyholes on the back and mark them precisely on the wall
  • Drive screws in at a slight upward angle so the sign locks in firmly

Outdoor Mounting Tips

  • Use stainless steel or coated screws for outdoor signs to prevent rust streaks
  • Pre-drill pilot holes to avoid splitting the wood frame or post
  • Leave a small gap between the sign and the mounting surface for airflow and water drainage
  • For fence mounting, use longer screws (2.5"+) to get through the fence boards
