Bridesmaid Gift Ideas 2025: What Orlando Brides Are Ordering

✍️ SieCraft Team 📅 2025 📍 Orlando, FL

We've made thousands of bridesmaid gifts for Orlando-area weddings. Here's what's trending in 2025 and what actually gets used after the wedding.

What Brides Order Most in 2025

  • Personalized tumblers — by far the most popular. Each bridesmaid's name on a matching 20oz powder-coated tumbler.
  • Custom cutting boards — names and wedding date. Popular for a combined bridal party/wedding gift.
  • Engraved jewelry dish — small laser-cut wood dish for rings and jewelry, personalized with her name.
  • Matching keychains — simple, elegant, and practical. Easy to personalize individually.
  • Mini wine glass — engraved with her name and the wedding date.

The Tumbler Trend Isn't Going Anywhere

Tumblers became the dominant bridesmaid gift around 2022 and they're still the top choice in 2025. Here's why: every woman uses a tumbler every day. It's not a trinket that sits on a shelf — it's something she carries, uses at work, and sees constantly. That daily use keeps the memory alive long after the wedding day.
